Synopsis

After a horrific alchemy experiment leaves brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric in broken, diminished forms, they spend years searching for the fabled Philosopher's Stone — the one thing that could restore what they've lost. Their journey pulls them into a vast conspiracy involving the military state of Amestris, a shadowy immortal called Father, and seven homunculi named after humanity's most destructive sins. Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood is a story about the cost of ambition, the limits of science, and the bonds that make us human.
